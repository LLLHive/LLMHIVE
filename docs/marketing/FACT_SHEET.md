# LLMHive Fact Sheet

## Overview
LLMHive is a multi‑model AI orchestration platform that routes complex tasks to specialized models, can cross‑verify outputs, and returns a single, coherent response.

## Why It Matters
- Reduces tool switching by routing each task to a suitable model.
- Can run multi‑model critique loops on high‑stakes prompts.
- Uses flat monthly plans instead of paying separately for every model.

## Benchmark Positioning
- #1 in 5 out of 8 benchmark categories (May 2026), as reported by LLMHive.
- Routes across 350+ OpenRouter models, including GPT‑5.6 Sol Pro, Claude Opus 5, Gemini 3.1 Pro, Grok 4.5, and Kimi K3.

## Typical Use Cases
- Legal research and contract analysis
- Financial research and reporting
- Healthcare and clinical summaries (not a HIPAA‑certified product)
- Customer support drafts
- Software development and code review
